Cruising Altitude

A focus timer where every work session is a flight

  • Matches a focus session's length to a real flight to one of about 175 cities worldwide.
  • Animates the flight live on a 3D globe, with a boarding pass, a seat map and a passport stamp when you land.
  • Keeps you coming back with daily goals, streaks, a flight log, and planes you unlock as you log hours.
